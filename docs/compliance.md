# Compliance notes (California Cottage Food Operation)

Jakki operates from Lincoln, CA (Placer County) under a CFO registration. Re-verify everything here with the agencies before launch; figures change yearly.

## What the law allows
- **Online direct sales** (AB 1144, effective 2022): orders by phone, internet or other digital means, fulfilled in person, by the operator, by mail or via third-party delivery — **within California only**.
- **Class A** (registration, self-certification, no routine inspection) covers direct sales only. Wholesale or café sales need **Class B** (permit + inspection).
- Annual gross sales caps adjust with CPI: $86,206 (Class A) / $172,411 (Class B) for 2025; ~$88k Class A expected for 2026 — confirm the official CDPH figure.

## What the site must show
- "Made in a Home Kitchen", the county (Placer) and the registration number on advertising, including the website (AB 831): footer + every product page.
- Ingredients and allergens for every product.
- Packaging labels (separate from the site): "Made in a Home Kitchen" in 12-point type, registration number, ingredients, allergens.
